# Writeup Title

## Challenge/Target summary

_What was the challenge/box/target? Category, difficulty, platform, and the
high-level goal._

## Recon

_What did initial enumeration turn up? Ports, services, endpoints, technologies
in use — and the reasoning behind what to look at next._

## Vulnerability/root cause

_What was actually broken, and why? Explain the underlying flaw, not just
"this was the bug."_

## Exploitation steps

_Concrete steps taken to exploit the vulnerability, with commands/payloads and
what each one accomplished._

## Remediation/what a fix would look like

_If this were a real system, what would the fix be? Patch, config change,
architectural change — and any tradeoffs._

## Lessons learned

_What did this teach me? What would I look for earlier next time, and what
generalizes beyond this specific target?_
